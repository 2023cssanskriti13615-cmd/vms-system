Every visitor invited, verified and logged at the gate.

An employee sends the invitation, the visitor fills in their details from the emailed link, and a QR gate pass is issued. Security scans that pass at the gate, and the entry is written to the visitor register the moment it is approved.

Sign in How it works

  1. Invitation

    The employee enters the visitor's email, whom they are meeting and when. The system creates a random token that cannot be guessed.

  2. Visitor details

    The visitor opens the emailed link and fills in name, phone, company and purpose of the visit.

  3. Gate pass

    A QR code carrying that token is generated and emailed to the visitor as a single-use gate pass.

  4. Gate check

    Security scans the code. The system confirms the token, shows who the visitor is, and records the entry time once entry is approved.

Who signs in here

Employees invite visitors and view the register. Security officers verify passes at the gate. Visitors never need an account — their invitation link is their access.
